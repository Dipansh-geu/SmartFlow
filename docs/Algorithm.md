# Density Based Traffic Signal Algorithm

## Step 1

Generate vehicles for each lane.

## Step 2

Insert vehicles into lane queues.

## Step 3

Calculate queue length of all lanes.

## Step 4

Find lane with maximum density.

## Step 5

Check starvation counter.

## Step 6

Allocate green signal.

## Step 7

Allow vehicles to pass.

## Step 8

Update waiting time statistics.

## Step 9

Repeat until simulation ends.
